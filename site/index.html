From bag to sink.

  1. Pack both heads flat

    Lay both heads in the half with two spring arms. Rest the shafts against the curved sidewalls, with the round ends at opposite ends of the case and the bristles facing inward. Press the lid until the snap engages.

  2. Stand them upright

    Lift at the front recess and open the case flat on a level counter. Set one head on each peg. Slots carry water from beneath each heel to outlets at the short ends.

Print it flat.

Import the STL as one object at 100% in millimeters. Keep the supplied orientation with both halves flat and the pegs facing up. Leave supports and brim off.

Let the plate cool before lifting the case and gently folding it for the first time.
